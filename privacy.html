Sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker)

  • Divine Guide questions, when you use the guide without your own key: the question and recent chat turns go to the worker, which forwards them to Google Gemini for an answer. They are not stored by us.
  • Panchang reminders, if you turn on a reminder bell: a push subscription (a browser-generated address, no name or email) and the reminder dates are kept so the reminder can be sent.
  • Sankalp+ free trial: when you start the 7-day trial, the app sends a random 32-character device ID that it made up (not linked to your name, phone or account). The server does not store that ID. It stores a one-way hash of it with the trial's start date, end date and whether the one-time 7-day extension was used, so each device gets one trial. This record is kept for about 13 months and then deleted automatically. The token in your browser is signed by the server and checked about once a day.
  • Trial-end question: if you answer "Would you pay for this?", your answer is added to anonymous totals, counted by your browser's language setting (for example en-IN), your country (worked out by Cloudflare from the connection, stored as a two-letter code only) and the city you chose in the app (from the app's city list). Your email, name, IP address and device ID are never stored with an answer, and answers are not kept as individual records or with a time. Separately, your device's hashed trial record notes that the one-time extension was used; it does not record what you answered.
  • Redeem codes, if someone gives you one: a random 32-character key (not linked to your name) with the plan and expiry date.

Like any website, our hosting providers (Vercel and Cloudflare) see your IP address when you connect. We do not store it; a short-lived in-memory counter used to limit abuse is discarded within minutes.

City muhurat, the Diwali guide and festival calendar files are computed in your browser. Location for "Nearby stores" is used only in your browser to build a map link.

Payments

Divine Hub takes no payments. Sankalp+ is offered as a free trial; no card or bank details are requested, and nothing is charged when the trial ends. If paid plans are introduced later, this page will be updated first, and checkout would be handled by the payment provider on its own pages.

If you email us, we see your email address and request ID, used only to reply.

How to delete your data

On this device: the button below clears every Divine Hub key from this browser and its offline cache. You can also use your browser's "clear site data". Note that this removes Sankalp+ from this device too, including the trial device ID in IndexedDB.

On our server: reminder subscriptions are removed automatically once reminders fire or the browser unsubscribes. Trial records are deleted automatically after about 13 months. Anonymous answer totals cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing personal to delete. To delete a trial record sooner, or anything else, email ankitsamriwal@gmail.com with your request ID (shown on the Sankalp+ sheet).
