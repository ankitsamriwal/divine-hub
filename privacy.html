Sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker)

  • Divine Guide questions, when you use the guide without your own key: the question and recent chat turns go to the worker, which forwards them to Google Gemini for an answer. They are not stored by us.
  • Panchang reminders, if you turn on a reminder bell: a push subscription (a browser-generated address, no name or email) and the reminder dates are kept so the reminder can be sent.
  • Sankalp+: a random 32-character key (not linked to your name) and your plan and expiry date, so a payment can be matched to this device. The token in your browser is signed by the server and checked about once a day.

City muhurat, the Diwali guide and festival calendar files are computed in your browser. Location for "Nearby stores" is used only in your browser to build a map link.

Payments

Sankalp+ checkout is handled by Stripe (outside India) or Razorpay (India) on their own pages. Card and bank details go only to them, under their privacy policies; Divine Hub never sees them. We receive a confirmation that a payment for your random key succeeded, with plan and amount. Your email may be visible to us in the provider's dashboard if the provider collects it for your receipt.

If you ask for access by email, we see your email address and request ID, used only to reply.

How to delete your data

On this device: the button below clears every Divine Hub key from this browser and its offline cache. You can also use your browser's "clear site data". Note that this removes Sankalp+ from this device too; keep your restore key first if you are a subscriber (Sankalp+ sheet, "Use Plus on another device").

On our server: reminder subscriptions are removed automatically once reminders fire or the browser unsubscribes. To delete Sankalp+ records, or anything else, email ankitsamriwal@gmail.com with your restore key or request ID. To cancel a subscription, use the link in your Stripe or Razorpay receipt.
